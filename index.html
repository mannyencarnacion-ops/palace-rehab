Fitchburg
Used To
Gather Here

Built in 1900, the Palace building spent generations as the neighborhood's gathering place — most recently as the Palace bowling lanes, where half the city learned to keep score.

Now it gets its next century. A full rehab is underway to bring the building back to life: restored, modernized, and opened up for the businesses and events that will write Cleghorn's next chapter.
