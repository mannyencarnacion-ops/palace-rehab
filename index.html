The building

One building, doing several different jobs at once.

It was a landmark on this corner for generations. The renovation keeps what made it worth keeping — the brick, the timber, the scale — and puts the floors back to work, each one carrying a different part of the same idea.

Renovated in phases. Parts of the building will open before others.

A community floor

Offices and meeting rooms for organizations serving this neighborhood, and quiet rooms for sitting down with someone one to one.

A floor to move on

Recreation, fitness, youth programming and a working kitchen — the everyday reasons to come through the door.

An event floor

The hall. Built for the celebrations, fundraisers and gatherings that fill a room, with a bar and a kitchen behind it.

A wedding on the event floor helps pay for the programs downstairs.

That is the whole model in one sentence. The parts of the building that earn are what keep the rest of it affordable — below-market rooms for local organizations, and programs a neighborhood can actually reach. It is why the hall matters as much as the offices.

Host an event

A room big enough for the whole guest list.

Weddings, quinceañeras, galas, conferences, banquets, showers, memorials, community fundraisers and concerts. One hall, a bar, a kitchen, and parking on a corner people can find.

Bookings will run through a single system, managed on site, so one person can answer you about dates, the room, the kitchen and the bar without sending you somewhere else.

Dates are not open yet. Tell us what you are planning and when, and you will hear from someone as soon as the calendar opens.

What is inside

A building with a lot going on in it.

Not one thing. The point of a building this size is that a lot of different reasons to come through the door can share the same roof, and pay for each other.

Some of it is open to anyone who walks in. Some of it belongs to the organizations based here. All of it is on the same corner.

Under this roof

  • An event hall
  • Bowling
  • A boxing gym
  • Fitness and personal training
  • A working kitchen
  • A café and lounge
  • Community meeting rooms
  • Offices for local organizations

Ask about the building

About the organizations here

The organizations based in this building run their own programs and their own services. Each one speaks for itself — what it does, who it serves and how to reach it are its own to describe, not this building's.

What this page can tell you is that they will be here, and how to get to the door.

Who is behind it

Organizations are already committed.

Reserved — not yet published

The partner organizations are not named on this page yet, on purpose.

Each one has to say yes, in writing, to how it is described here before its name appears in public. Agreeing to take part in a project is not the same as agreeing to appear on a website, and this page will not get ahead of that.

Once each organization has approved its own wording, this becomes the strongest section on the page — real names, real work, no photography required.
